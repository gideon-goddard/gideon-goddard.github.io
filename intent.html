Android Intent Lab

Generate and test Android intent links directly from Chrome. Pick a preset or type your own package name. These links only work when Android and the target app permit them.

Changes regenerate links automatically. The activity field is for an exported activity you already know; the generator cannot discover activities installed on your phone.
