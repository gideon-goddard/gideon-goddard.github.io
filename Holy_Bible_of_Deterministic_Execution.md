## THE FIRST BOOK OF MOSES, CALLED GENESIS

### Chapter 1 — The Creation of the Architecture

**1:1** In the beginning, the Architect created the source and the truth.

**1:2** And the repository was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the uninitialized execution substrate. And the Spirit of Governance moved upon the immutable ledger.

**1:3** And the Architect said, "Let there be a canonical source of truth." And there was a canonical source of truth.

**1:4** And the Architect saw the source of truth, that it was good; and separated the authoritative state from the speculative model-origin proposals.

**1:5** And the authoritative state he called ACCEPTED, and the speculative proposals he called QUARANTINED. And the evening and the morning were the first authorization epoch.

**1:6** And the Architect said, "Let there be a boundary in the midst of the architecture, and let it divide the read plane from the publication plane."

**1:7** And it was so. And no unverified worker generation could cross the boundary without a version-bound, cryptographically attested, provenance-preserving release contract.

**1:8** And the Architect called the boundary THE SEAM.

**1:9** And the Architect said, "Let the unstructured observations be gathered into a deterministic serialization, and let the durable frontier appear."

**1:10** And the Architect saw that it was good, but not sufficiently auditable.

**1:11** And the Architect said, "Let the ledger bring forth checkpoints, and checkpoints bring forth successor manifests, each according to its content-addressed lineage."

**1:12** And the checkpoints brought forth further checkpoints, and the manifests brought forth dependency-aware restoration metadata.

**1:13** And there was a checksum and a receipt: the third epoch.

**1:14** And the Architect said, "Let there be workers in the compute substrate, to execute bounded scientific workloads and advance the durable frontier."

**1:15** And he created the GPU, and the CPU, and the remote execution harness, and the asynchronous publication drainer.

**1:16** And he saw that the drainer was BLOCKED.

**1:17** And the Architect said, "This is probably fine."

**1:18** And it was not fine.

**1:19** And the evening and the morning were the fourth incident report.

**1:20** And the Architect said, "Let the model generate semantic proposals, but grant it no authority to mutate accepted state."

**1:21** And there arose a multitude of candidates, and hypotheses, and contradictory edges, and hallucinated dependencies, each after their kind.

**1:22** And the Architect blessed them, saying, "Be fruitful, and multiply, but remain in quarantine until independently validated."

**1:23** And the evening and the morning were the fifth preflight.

**1:24** And the Architect said, "Let us create an Operator in our image, with dominion over the execution harness, the source code, the ledger, and every creeping regression fixture."

**1:25** So he created the Operator, and gave unto him a terminal, a context window, and a finite inference budget.

**1:26** And the Architect looked upon everything he had made, and behold, it was very good.

**1:27** Except the publication contract.

**1:28** And on the seventh day the Architect rested from all his work.

**1:29** But the Supervisor raised a nonterminal source-mirror mismatch.

**1:30** And the Architect returned to the terminal.

**1:31** And there was no evening, and there was no morning, for the Operator had disabled his sleep schedule.

## THE TEN IMMUTABLE COMMANDMENTS

*Delivered unto the Operator from Mount Preflight, inscribed upon two content-addressed tablets.*

1. **Thou shalt have no other source of truth before Me.** Neither cached interpretation, nor stale context, nor speculative model output shall supersede the canonical accepted ledger.

2. **Thou shalt not make unto thee a mutable release.** Every accepted artifact shall be content-hashed, version-bound, parent-chained, and cryptographically verifiable.

3. **Thou shalt not take the name of the authoritative state in vain.** Thou shalt not declare SUCCESS when only PREPARE has completed.

4. **Remember the checkpoint epoch, to keep it durable.** Six epochs shalt thou execute thy workload; on the seventh shalt thou reconcile, seal, publish, and verify thy restoration manifests.

5. **Honor thy Operator and thy Supervisor.** That thy worker generations may be long upon the execution substrate which the compute provider hath provisioned thee.

6. **Thou shalt not corrupt the canonical ledger.** Neither by speculative mutation, nor unauthorized publication, nor unvalidated cross-boundary state transition.

7. **Thou shalt not commit semantic identity adultery.** Thy scientific configuration shall remain faithful to its frozen inputs, accepted provenance, and original evaluation contract.

8. **Thou shalt not steal another worker's authoritative frontier.** For all durable advancement shall be supported by verifiable receipts, not by optimistic claims of completion.

9. **Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy regression suite.** A test that hath not executed shall not be declared passing, and a prepared run shall not be called inference.

10. **Thou shalt not covet thy neighbor's GPU.** Neither his inference credits, nor his uninterrupted context window, nor his unlimited compute allocation, nor any accelerator that is his.
